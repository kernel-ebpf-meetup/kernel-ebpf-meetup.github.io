About

The meetup runs in a "Do It Yourself" style: presentation is optional, and all formats are welcome. Bring a talk, a demo, a patch you are stuck on, or just questions. There are no scheduled talks unless someone wants to give one, and most of the time goes to open discussion and comparing notes on what people are working on.

Locations change from month to month and are announced on the calendar ahead of each meetup.

  • Do It Yourself. Presentation is optional; talks, demos, and any other format are welcome.
  • Open discussion. Share experiences, ask questions, learn together.
  • Networking. Connect with other kernel and eBPF developers.
  • Hands-on. Bring your laptop for impromptu coding sessions.
  • All levels welcome. From curious beginners to kernel maintainers.

What we discuss

Topics are driven by community interest and collected through our topic survey. Recurring subjects include:

  • Linux kernel internals and development
  • eBPF programming and use cases
  • Performance monitoring and tracing
  • Networking and XDP
  • Security and sandboxing with eBPF
  • Kernel debugging techniques
  • Latest kernel features and patches
  • Real-world production experiences
